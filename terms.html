Terms & Booking Policy

Last updated: September 2026

These terms cover how appointment booking works on this website and what to expect once you submit a request. For questions, use the contact details at the bottom of this page.

What a booking is

Submitting the booking form is a request for a consultation, not a confirmed contract. TRIGDA reviews each request and follows up using the email or phone number you provided to confirm, adjust, or discuss the proposed date and time.

What happens after you book

You'll see an on-screen confirmation immediately after a successful submission, and your request is stored right away. TRIGDA aims to respond to new booking requests within one to two business days.

Rescheduling

If your preferred date or time no longer works, contact TRIGDA using the email or phone number on the Contact page and mention your original preferred date. TRIGDA will confirm an updated time with you directly.

Cancellations

You can cancel a pending or upcoming consultation at any time by emailing or calling TRIGDA. There is no fee for cancelling a free consultation.

Accuracy of information

Please provide accurate contact details and a clear description of your project or issue. Incomplete or incorrect contact information may delay confirmation.

Pricing

Service pricing is not published on this website, since scope varies by project. Pricing is discussed and agreed directly with you after your consultation.

Communication

By submitting the booking form and agreeing to the consent checkbox, you agree that TRIGDA may contact you using the details you provided, for the purpose of scheduling and delivering the service you requested. See the Privacy Policy for how your information is handled.

Fair use of the chatbot

The TRIGDA Assistant chatbot is provided to help answer common questions about services, process, and booking. It is limited to a set number of messages per visitor within a rolling window, and it will hand off to a human contact for anything outside its approved knowledge base.

Changes to these terms

TRIGDA may update this policy from time to time to reflect how the booking process actually works. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent change.
