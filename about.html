About TRIGDA

TRIGDA is a technology and automation partner based in Gujranwala, Pakistan, built around one idea: the parts of your business that repeat should run by themselves.

How TRIGDA works

Founded by Talha Bin Saeed, TRIGDA takes on the practical, unglamorous work behind a growing business - the website that needs to actually convert, the prospect list that needs to actually be accurate, and the follow-up messages that need to actually go out on time. Instead of treating these as separate vendors, TRIGDA builds them to work together as one system.

That means every project starts with understanding how your business runs today, not a generic template. Websites are built to support real forms and real integrations. Lead generation is scoped to the customers you actually want, not a broad list. Automation is designed around the specific tasks eating your team's time.

What TRIGDA values

  • Direct, fast communication - you should never be guessing where a project stands.
  • Automation-first thinking - if something repeats, it's a candidate to automate.
  • Custom solutions - built for your business, not a one-size-fits-all package.

Approach

Every engagement follows the same five steps: understand the requirement, plan the solution, build and configure it, test it thoroughly, then launch and support it. That structure keeps projects predictable regardless of whether it's a website, a lead campaign, or an automation system.
