Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information TRIGDA collects through this website, why, and how long it's kept. If anything here is unclear, contact TRIGDA using the details at the bottom of this page.

What we collect

When you book an appointment, we collect your full name, email address, phone number, company name, the service you're interested in, your preferred date and time, and the project or issue details you provide. If you use the chatbot, we store your messages and the assistant's replies for the duration needed to answer your questions and keep the conversation working.

Why we collect it

Appointment details are collected solely to process and respond to your booking request - to understand what you need, confirm a time, and follow up with you. Chatbot messages are used only to answer your questions within the current session.

Data minimization

We only ask for what's needed to respond to your request. We don't ask for sensitive information such as payment details, national ID numbers, or passwords through the booking form or chatbot, and you should never enter that information into either.

Consent

The booking form includes a consent checkbox. By checking it and submitting the form, you agree that TRIGDA may contact you using the details you provided, for the purpose of responding to your request.

Retention

  • Appointment records are retained for as long as needed to deliver the service and for a reasonable period afterward for business record-keeping, after which they may be deleted or anonymized.
  • Chatbot conversation transcripts are not kept longer than operationally necessary and are not used for purposes beyond answering your questions and improving the assistant's approved knowledge base.
  • Security logs (such as login attempts and booking events) are retained for a limited period to protect the website against abuse and unauthorized access.

How your data is protected

Booking and account data is stored in a database that is not publicly accessible. Administrative access requires authentication, and passwords are never stored in plain text. The website uses HTTPS in production so data submitted through forms is encrypted in transit.

Who can see your information

Booking details are visible only to authorized TRIGDA staff through a protected admin area. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties except where needed to operate the service (for example, sending you an email confirmation) or where required by law.

Your choices

You can ask TRIGDA about what information we hold about you, request a correction, or request deletion, subject to any legal or legitimate business record-keeping requirements.
